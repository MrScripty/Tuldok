# Training Evaluation Research Paper

This package contains a principles-first educational research chapter on evaluating and visually understanding model training runs. It begins with inputs, targets, objectives, updates, and held-out evidence, then develops practical diagnostics and a substantial three-dimensional fluid and pressure-surrogate case.

## Read the paper

- [Main chapter](evaluating-and-visually-understanding-training-runs.md)
- [Original publications and source-to-claim bibliography](bibliography.md)
- [Numerical data for authored illustrations](illustrations-data.json)

The three figures in `figures/` are self-contained SVG files linked from the chapter. Keep that folder beside the chapter when copying the source into a repository. The Markdown uses GitHub-compatible inline and display math delimiters.

## Evidence boundaries

The literature was checked on 15 October 2026. The bibliography contains 43 original research or methodological publications and one separately labeled pinned project execution report. The report was read, not independently rerun. Its negative numerical result and prior test exposure remain explicit.

The diagrams, score sheets, arithmetic examples, conditional pressure projection calculation, and exact forced-flow teaching case are authored illustrations. They are not measured model-training results. Their values do not prescribe universal physical tolerances. No local project source, data, training recipe, or repository was modified to create this paper.

## Contents and learning route

Sections 1–5 establish beginner principles and fixed checkpoint probes. Sections 6–9 explain metrics, learning curves, optimizer diagnostics, and truthful visualization. Sections 10–13 develop pressure, physical constraints, rollout, and uncertainty. Sections 14–16 cover human scoring, learning mechanisms, fair comparisons, timing, and diagnostic decisions. Sections 17–19 provide staged exercises, a practical checklist, and research gaps.

The chapter and bibliography can stand alone as a research tutorial. They can also be incorporated as a longer book chapter with the same supporting figures. No executable training scripts are required to follow the examples.
